Historical Evolution of Experimental Movements

Explore the foundational eras that broke cinematic rules first, from 1920s Surrealism to Structural Film.

The 1920s European Avant-Garde (Dadaism & Surrealism): Emerging in post-WWI Europe, this movement rejected rational bourgeois logic and narrative cause-and-effect. Influenced by psychoanalysis, artists sought to bypass the conscious mind entirely, using jarring juxtapositions, dream-states, and shocking imagery (such as the razor-blade eye slice) to unlock the subconscious. Landmark Work: Un Chien Andalou (Luis Buñuel & Salvador Dalí, 1929).
